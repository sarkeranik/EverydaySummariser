👋 Welcome, Adventurer!
Everyday Summariser automatically captures your daily browsing and turns it into an AI-powered journal.
  • 📝TEXT Captures clean article text from every page
  • 🖼️IMAGES Records image URLs for visual context
  • 🎵AUDIO Records audio from tabs playing sound
  • 🎬YOUTUBE Extracts video transcripts automatically
  • 📄PDF Captures text from PDFs in the browser
  • 🐦TWITTER Extracts thread text from Twitter/X
💡 Everything stays local on your machine. Your data never leaves your computer.
🖥️ Backend Setup
The extension needs a local Python backend to store data and generate summaries.
Run one of these commands in the project root:

Docker: docker-compose up -d
Without Docker: Run start_windows.bat or start_mac.sh

🤖 AI Configuration
Choose which AI will generate your daily journal entries.
💡 The AI Provider is determined by how you started the backend script (Terminal).
💡 You can change this later in Settings from the Dashboard.
🔒 Privacy Settings
Control what gets captured. Add domains you want to exclude.
💡 Pages on blocked domains will NOT be captured. Good for banking, email, and private sites.
You can always update this in Settings later.
🎮
You're All Set!
Start browsing normally. The extension will silently capture your activity.
Click the extension icon to generate your daily note anytime!
